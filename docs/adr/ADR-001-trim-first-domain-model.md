# ADR-001: Trim-first domain model

Status: Accepted — 2026-08-22

All prices, features, specifications, colors and comparisons resolve to a market trim inside a model-year/generation context. Model-level facts may be retained only when their scope is explicit and must not be copied to Vietnam trims without evidence.

This prevents different variants from sharing incorrect price/equipment and supplies a stable identity for history and provenance.
